The Personal Me

Beyond the pipelines.

The engineer builds the systems. This is the person behind them: the curiosity, the taste, and everything that happens away from the keyboard.

01 · My Story

I'm Odiche, Odi to most people. I build intelligent systems by day, and chase good ideas the rest of the time.

My curiosity has never really respected job descriptions. The same instinct that makes me want to understand how a model reasons is the one that pulls me toward a new city, an unfamiliar recipe, or a camera I haven't quite figured out yet.

I grew up moving between cultures, and I think that's why I'm drawn to things that bridge worlds: technology and craft, data and story, the analytical and the human. I like taking something complicated and making it feel simple, considered, and a little beautiful.

When I'm not shipping pipelines, I'm usually somewhere with headphones on: planning the next trip, cooking something from home, or tinkering with a side project that probably didn't need to exist. This page is the unedited version of me.
