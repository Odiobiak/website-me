Multimodal AI Agent for Auto Insurance Quotes

Problem

A Fortune 500 insurer wanted a new auto quote customers could start in chat, finish by voice and complete with a document instead of a form, built well enough to become its production template.

Approach

A hybrid agent on NICE Cognigy with GPT-4.1: the model handles conversation, while a versioned rules layer (79 fields) handles validation, guardrails and routing. Chat and browser voice share one session; uploads are read by vision; prefill, VIN and rating plug in as subflows.

Impact

Customers answer only what's missing, switch channels freely, and the build ships with its own regression suite.

Personal AI Agents over the Personal Agent Protocol

Problem

Customers' AI assistants are starting to fill in forms, chat and call for them, mostly posing as the customer. Companies can't tell who is acting, for whom, or with what consent.

Approach

Built both sides over PAP 0.2: a GPT-4.1 assistant with its own signing key, and a fictional insurer's front door that verifies it (signed assertions, DPoP-bound tokens), signs the customer in by device code, and routes to an AI agent by conversation or MCP tools, behind the same rules.

Results

One sentence from the customer produced a full quote: about 20 exchanges and 46 saved answers. Forged identities and stolen tokens never reached the agent.

Agentic AI

Multi-Agent Rebate Q&A Assistant

Problem

Client teams spent hours answering rebate questions from scattered, inconsistent enterprise data.

Approach

A LangChain orchestrator routes to a retrieval agent (hybrid search over Pinecone), a metadata-quality agent and a GPT-4o answer agent, wrapped in guardrails, retries and checkpointing behind a Streamlit UI.

Impact

Hours of Q&A became near-instant, with structured, auditable answers evaluated at scale.

Metadata Quality Agents

Problem

Inconsistent enterprise metadata caused integration errors and a heavy manual review load.

Approach

Evaluation agents (tool calling, structured outputs) score records against rule checks and an LLM judge; confident records publish to the catalog, the rest go to human review.

Impact

Cleaner, catalog-ready metadata, with people in the loop only where it counts.

Real-Time Traffic Data Pipeline

Problem

Real-time traffic data had to feed risk models and dashboards reliably across environments.

Approach

Kafka ingests events, Airflow orchestrates, PySpark validates and cleans, and a Postgres warehouse serves risk models and dashboards across Prod, Stage and Dev.
