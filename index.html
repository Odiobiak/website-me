04 · Case Studies

Case studies in production.

Three systems I designed and shipped end-to-end, with the architecture behind each. Agentic AI, data engineering, and enterprise automation.

Agentic AI

Multi-Agent Rebate Q&A Assistant

Problem

Client teams spent hours answering rebate questions from scattered, inconsistent enterprise data.

Approach

A multi-agent RAG system: a LangChain orchestrator routes to a retrieval agent (hybrid search over Pinecone), a metadata-quality evaluation agent, and a GPT-4o answer agent, wrapped in guardrails, retries, and checkpointing behind a Streamlit UI.

Impact

Rebate Q&A that used to take hours became near-instant, with structured, auditable answers evaluated at scale.

~60% faster Q&AAuditable answersEvaluated at scale
Data Engineering

Real-Time Traffic Data Pipeline

Problem

Real-time traffic data had to feed risk models and dashboards reliably, consistently across environments.

Approach

A streaming ETL pipeline: Kafka ingests events, Airflow orchestrates, PySpark validates and cleans, and a Postgres warehouse serves risk models and dashboards, promoted across Prod/Stage/Dev.

Impact

A dependable real-time platform with far fewer errors and dramatically less manual handling.

35% fewer errors60% less manual workReal-time throughput
Enterprise Automation

Metadata Quality Agents

Problem

Inconsistent enterprise metadata caused integration errors and a heavy manual review load.

Approach

Task-specific evaluation agents (tool/function calling + structured outputs) score metadata against rule checks and an LLM judge; high-confidence records publish to the catalog while low-confidence ones route to human review. Automated across environments.

Impact

Cleaner, catalog-ready metadata with humans kept in the loop only where it counts.
